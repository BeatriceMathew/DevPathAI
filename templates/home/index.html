FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About DevPath AI?

Find answers to common questions about personalized learning, skill analysis and career roadmaps.

What is DevPath AI?

DevPath AI is an AI-powered learning platform that analyzes your current skills and career goals to create a personalized developer learning roadmap.

How does the AI skill analysis work?

The system compares your existing skills with the skills required for your selected career role and identifies the areas you need to improve.

Can I choose my career goal?

Yes. You can select a target career such as Java Developer, Python Developer, Full Stack Developer, AI/ML Developer, Data Scientist or DevOps Engineer.

Can I track my learning progress?

Yes. Your dashboard will show completed skills, pending tasks and your overall roadmap completion.

Will DevPath AI recommend projects?

Yes. Based on your career goal and skill level, DevPath AI can recommend projects that help you practice the skills in your roadmap.

Is the roadmap the same for every student?

No. The roadmap is personalized according to the student's current skills, experience and selected career goal.
